1. Quality Promise

At Kumo Ramen, our broths are slow-simmered for 18 hours daily using fresh ingredients. We do not use any artificial preservatives. Once prepared, orders should be consumed within 2 hours to ensure freshness.

2. Ordering & Deliveries

We deliver within a 5km radius of our kitchens. Standard preparation times vary between 15-25 minutes, with delivery times ranging from 30-45 minutes. Pre-orders are cooked to be ready at the scheduled slot.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

Due to the perishable nature of our fresh ramen, orders cannot be cancelled once preparation has started. In case of damaged deliveries or incorrect items, please contact support with details within 30 minutes of receipt for refunds.

4. Allergy Warnings

Our kitchen processes wheat, egg, soy, seafood, peanuts, sesame, and dairy. Please consult our allergens guide or speak to a chef before placing orders if you have severe dietary sensitivities.
